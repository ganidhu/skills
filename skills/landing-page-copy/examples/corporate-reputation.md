# Corporate Reputation Homepages

## Use when

Use for holding companies, parent companies, conglomerates, global employers, and corporate groups.

## Primary belief

> This organization is legitimate, substantial, responsibly managed, and relevant to my stakeholder relationship.

## Structure

1. Mission and identity.
2. Behavioral operating principles.
3. Portfolio or business units.
4. History and milestones.
5. Scale and footprint.
6. Governance and stakeholder contact routes.

## Copy rules

- Serve employees, media, investors, suppliers, regulators, and partners.
- Separate parent-company identity from product-brand messaging.
- Use scale only when its implication is clear.
- Make governance visible.

## Failure modes

- Turning a corporate homepage into an irrelevant product catalog.
- Vague values with no behavior.
- Scale claims without history or evidence.
- Hiding stakeholder contact paths.
