# Category-Creation Landing Pages

## Use when

Use when the company is introducing a new mental model, replacing an old architecture, or establishing a new product category.

## Primary belief

> The old way is structurally inadequate, the new category solves the underlying problem, and this company is credible enough to define it.

## Structure

1. Name the category.
2. Establish the old-world problem.
3. Define the new mechanism.
4. Create a memorable operating model.
5. Show the system components.
6. Prove operational value.
7. Invite exploration.

## Copy rules

- Contrast old and new clearly.
- Define unfamiliar terms immediately.
- Use category language consistently.
- Prove that the category exists in production.
- Move from abstraction to operational detail.

## Failure modes

- Naming a category without explaining it.
- Inventing jargon faster than the audience can learn it.
- Claiming transformation without a before-and-after story.
- Focusing on internal architecture instead of customer change.
