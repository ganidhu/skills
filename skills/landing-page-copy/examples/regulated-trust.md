# Regulated-Trust Landing Pages

## Use when

Use for financial infrastructure, banking, healthcare, defense, insurance, identity, cybersecurity, and other risk-sensitive categories.

## Primary belief

> This opportunity is valuable, and the company can deliver it without exposing us to unacceptable risk.

## Structure

1. Opportunity plus control.
2. Core capabilities.
3. Security, compliance, governance, auditability, and risk controls.
4. Institutional proof.
5. Professional next step.

## Copy rules

- Pair major innovation claims with control claims.
- Be precise about guarantees, measurements, and intentions.
- Keep required disclosures visible.
- Use proof from organizations resembling the buyer.

## Failure modes

- Treating security as a footer badge.
- Vague trust language.
- Hiding important limitations.
- Overpromising results.
