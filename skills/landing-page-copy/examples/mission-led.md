# Mission-Led Landing Pages

## Use when

Use for frontier technology, scientific companies, climate companies, space companies, public-benefit organizations, and businesses tied to a long-term future.

## Primary belief

> The future this company describes matters, and the company has a credible way to help create it.

## Structure

1. Memorable future state.
2. Why the future matters.
3. Mechanism: products, technologies, research, or operating choices.
4. Evidence of movement.
5. Participation paths for customers, candidates, partners, or followers.

## Copy rules

- Make the vision memorable.
- Explain why it matters.
- Connect mission to mechanism.
- Use milestones to prove movement.
- Avoid generic purpose language.

## Failure modes

- Grand mission with no operational evidence.
- Inspiration without explaining what the company does.
- Generic values.
- High-commitment CTA before belief is established.
