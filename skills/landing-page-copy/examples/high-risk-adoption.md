# High-Risk Adoption Pages

## Use when

Use for autonomous vehicles, medical technology, security products, robotics, unfamiliar financial products, and products where visitors fear loss of control or personal harm.

## Primary belief

> I understand enough about how this works, and the evidence is strong enough for me to try it safely.

## Structure

1. Emotional reassurance.
2. Simple mechanism.
3. Quantified safety or reliability.
4. Real-world scenarios.
5. User experience and relevant testimonials.
6. Availability and reversible trial.

## Copy rules

- Resolve the strongest fear early.
- Use evidence rather than reassurance alone.
- Explain methodology where claims are consequential.
- Let users assess availability before committing.
- Show the product working in difficult conditions.

## Failure modes

- Vague emotional headline with no explanation.
- Safety information buried below the fold.
- Technical proof without lived experience.
- Full commitment before a trial or availability check.
