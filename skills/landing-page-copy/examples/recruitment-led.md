# Recruitment-Led Landing Pages

## Use when

Use when the page's main job is attracting a specific employee, researcher, engineer, operator, or mission-driven collaborator.

## Primary belief

> This is meaningful, difficult work, and the company is structured so the right person can do it well.

## Structure

1. Mission.
2. Nature of the work.
3. Operating model: focus, autonomy, standards, pace, and constraints.
4. Who will thrive.
5. Invitation to join or apply.

## Copy rules

- Filter as well as attract.
- Be specific about the work.
- Avoid generic culture language.
- Sell meaning, mastery, autonomy, or impact only when genuine.
- Do not hide hard constraints.

## Failure modes

- Listing perks instead of work.
- Saying the company is a family.
- Mission language without operating reality.
- Attracting everyone and filtering no one.
