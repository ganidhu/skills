# Consumer Ecosystem Landing Pages

## Use when

Use for telecom companies, digital banks, super-apps, media bundles, retail ecosystems, and consumer platforms offering multiple related services.

## Primary belief

> This company can solve my immediate need and become more useful across other parts of my daily life.

## Structure

1. Immediate task or offer.
2. Clear price, duration, benefits, limits, and conditions.
3. Core product experience.
4. Adjacent ecosystem.
5. Local relevance and personalization.
6. Support and service recovery.

## Copy rules

- Prioritize task completion.
- Use practical language.
- Make offers scannable.
- Separate urgent action from discovery content.
- Do not bury price, availability, or conditions.

## Failure modes

- Making a simple transaction feel like a corporate tour.
- Listing the entire ecosystem before the main action.
- Lifestyle language that hides commercial terms.
- Too many competing promotions.
