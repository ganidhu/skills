# Enterprise Infrastructure Landing Pages

## Use when

Use for data platforms, payment infrastructure, cloud systems, developer platforms, security infrastructure, and complex B2B products with multiple stakeholders.

## Primary belief

> This platform can produce a meaningful business outcome and is safe, capable, and flexible enough to adopt at our scale.

## Structure

1. Business-outcome hero.
2. Plain-language platform explanation.
3. Capability-to-outcome sections.
4. Reliability, security, and scale proof.
5. Audience routing for executives, operators, and developers.
6. Adoption paths such as no-code, API, integrations, migration, and sales consultation.

## Copy rules

- Lead with business consequence.
- Translate architecture into workflow impact.
- Use metrics that correspond to buying risk.
- Make implementation and integration visible.
- Explain customer outcomes, not only customer logos.

## Failure modes

- Feature catalog without a business thesis.
- Generic claims about scale or security.
- One CTA for every audience.
- Technical jargon without plain-language explanation.
